Physical Significance of Wave Function

Understand what the wave function ψ means physically, why ψ itself is not directly observable, and how |ψ|² gives the probability density of finding a particle.

LEARN

Physical Significance of Wave Function

Born's interpretation of the wave function.

What is the wave function?

In quantum mechanics, the state of a microscopic particle is represented by a mathematical function called the wave function, denoted by ψ (psi).

The wave function contains the information needed to describe the quantum state of the particle. It may depend on position and time, so it can be written generally as ψ(x,t).

Born's Interpretation

The wave function itself does not directly represent a measurable physical quantity. The quantity |ψ|² represents the probability density of finding the particle at a particular position.
P(x) = |ψ(x)|²
Probability density at position x

Probability of finding the particle

If the particle is described by ψ(x), then the probability of finding it between x and x + dx is

dP = |ψ(x)|² dx

Therefore, the probability of finding the particle between two positions x₁ and x₂ is

P(x₁ ≤ x ≤ x₂) = ∫x₁x₂ |ψ(x)|² dx

Normalization Condition

Since the particle must be found somewhere in the allowed region, the total probability of finding it must be equal to one.

∫ |ψ|² dτ = 1
Integration is over the complete space.

A wave function satisfying this condition is said to be normalized.

Important observation

  • ψ may be positive, negative or complex.
  • |ψ|² is real and non-negative.
  • Large |ψ|² means a greater probability density.
  • Small |ψ|² means a smaller probability density.
  • At a node, ψ = 0 and therefore |ψ|² = 0.

SIGNIFICANCE

Physical Significance

What the wave function tells us—and what it does not.

Meaning of ψ

The wave function is a mathematical description of the quantum state. It is not itself directly observed in an experiment. Measurable predictions are obtained from appropriate quantities constructed from the wave function.

ψ

The probability amplitude. It may be positive, negative or complex and is not itself interpreted as a probability.

|ψ|²

The probability density. It is real, non-negative and gives the probability density of finding the particle.

Conditions for an acceptable wave function

  • It should be finite.
  • It should be single-valued.
  • It should be continuous where physically required.
  • Its first derivative should be continuous where the potential is finite.
  • It should be normalizable so that the total probability is finite and can be made equal to one.

Why normalization matters

Probability must add up to one because the particle must be found somewhere in the complete allowed space.

∫ |ψ|² dτ = 1

Quick Check

Test your understanding of ψ and |ψ|².

1. What is the physical significance of |ψ|²?

Correct: A. |ψ|² represents probability density.

2. The wave function ψ itself is:

Correct: B.

3. The total probability of finding a particle in all space is:

Correct: B. Hence the normalization condition ∫|ψ|²dτ = 1.

4. Can |ψ|² be negative?

Correct: B. Probability density is real and non-negative.

5. The probability of finding a particle between x₁ and x₂ is:

Correct: B. Probability is obtained by integrating the probability density.
